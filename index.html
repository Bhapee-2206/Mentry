CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about program formats, schedule rhythm, offline doubt sessions, and enrollment.

1. What are the programs offered? expand_more
Mentry Solutions currently offers two flagship technology programs: Java Development (Core Java, OOP, Collections, Exception Architecture, MySQL/JDBC databases, and capstone software application) and AI / ML (Python data engineering, NumPy matrices, Pandas dataframes, supervised Scikit-Learn models, evaluation metrics, and end-to-end predictive capstone pipeline).
2. Are classes live? expand_more
Yes, 100% of our daily sessions are live and instructor-led. You attend real-time virtual lectures with experienced technology practitioners who code and explain concepts live on screen, enabling immediate questions and real-time interaction.
3. How long is the program? expand_more
Each program runs for approximately 3 to 4 months. This duration provides enough runway to master fundamentals, practice core exercises, build database/pipeline workflows, and complete a comprehensive final capstone project.
4. How many days per week? expand_more
Online live lectures are scheduled 5 days per week. In addition, students have a dedicated weekly offline doubt clarification session at our designated center.
5. How long is each class? expand_more
Each live class lasts approximately 1 hour. This focused daily duration is purposefully calibrated to help students maintain high cognitive retention and consistency without clashing with university coursework or exam schedules.
6. Who can join? expand_more
The programs are open to college students pursuing degrees such as B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc Computer Science, MCA, as well as fresh graduates and beginners seeking structured, practical entry into software engineering or data science.
7. Is this suitable for freshers? expand_more
Yes, our programs are specifically designed with freshers and college students in mind. Modules begin with core logic building and fundamental syntax before advancing into complex application architecture.
8. Are classes recorded? expand_more
While live interactive attendance is strongly encouraged for retention and real-time practice, session recordings and reference code repositories are made available so you can revisit complex topics or catch up if you occasionally miss a class.
9. Is there a final project? expand_more
Yes. Both tracks conclude with a substantial capstone build. Java students deliver a multi-tier database application with clean architecture and transactional safety. AI/ML students train, tune, and evaluate an end-to-end predictive pipeline with verified performance metrics.
10. Will I receive a certificate? expand_more
Yes. Students who successfully fulfill attendance requirements, submit weekly problem sets, and complete their capstone project will be awarded an official Certificate of Completion issued by Mentry Solutions.
11. Is placement guaranteed? expand_more
No, placement is not guaranteed. At Mentry Solutions, we maintain strict transparency: completion of the program does not guarantee employment. We provide placement assistance and career-oriented preparation to help students approach opportunities with stronger skills and authentic project experience.
12. What is placement assistance? expand_more
Placement assistance includes 1:1 resume structuring, GitHub project portfolio optimization, technical mock interview drills, industry expectations coaching, and notification of entry-level hiring drives matching your skill set.
13. How does offline doubt support work? expand_more
Alongside online live daily classes, enrolled students have a scheduled in-person session once a week at our designated center. You can bring your laptop to debug complex code errors, review project architecture, and receive hands-on mentor guidance.
14. How do I enquire/enroll? expand_more
Fill out the Admission Enquiry form below or click "Enquire Now". Our academic team will get in touch with upcoming batch start dates, counseling slots, and enrollment details.
